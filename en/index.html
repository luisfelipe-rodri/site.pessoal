About

Administrative data, patents and networks for applied economics

I am an economist trained at the Federal University of Mato Grosso (UFMT), where I also completed a master’s degree in Applied Economics. I am currently an associate researcher at the Interdisciplinary Center for Energy Planning (NIPE) at the University of Campinas (UNICAMP) and affiliated with the São Paulo Center for Biogas and Bioproducts Studies (CP2B).

My work combines econometrics, data envelopment analysis (DEA) and complex network analysis to study technological innovation, agribusiness efficiency, judicial governance and the energy transition. I work mainly in R, with Brazilian administrative microdata such as RAIS, CAGED, POF, PNADC and ComexStat, as well as patent databases.

I also maintain and contribute to open-source packages and teaching materials, and I write about quantitative methods for a broader audience.

Research

Current project

FAPESP · Technical Training Fellowship

Identifying the Components of the Biogas Technological System and its Technological Development

Using a patent database covering 1976 to 2019, the project identifies the components of the biogas technological system. The period captures the worldwide expansion of scientific research, patenting and the installation of biogas production plants. The analysis uses patent citation networks to map technological trajectories.
